About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the Gran Sasso Science Institute (GSSI) in L'Aquila, Italy, where I work at the intersection of Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence.

I am interested in understanding what makes AI-enabled systems trustworthy, with particular attention to fairness, transparency, and the way people interact with and experience these technologies.

My current research focuses particularly on Generative AI, exploring topics such as fairness and bias, human-AI interaction, socio-technical quality assurance, and continuous monitoring of AI systems .

Before starting my Ph.D., I earned my Master's Degree in Computer Science at the University of Salerno, specializing in Software Engineering and IT Management, where I graduated cum laude.

Research

Research Interests

Ethical AI in Healthcare and Human-AI Interaction

Responsible, transparent, and trustworthy AI systems in medical contexts, with attention to human-centered decision-making and the interaction between AI-based tools and human stakeholders.

Software Engineering for Artificial Intelligence

Software engineering practices, methods, and tools for the development, evaluation, and maintenance of AI-based systems, with particular attention to trustworthy and explainable AI.

Empirical Software Engineering

Data-driven and experimental studies aimed at understanding software systems, development processes, and developers' practices through evidence-based methods.

Social Aspects in Software Engineering

Collaboration, coordination, and socio-technical factors in software development communities, including the interaction between technical decisions and social dynamics.
